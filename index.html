METHODOLOGY · SIGNAL MODEL
The intent-signal approach

Most lead-detection systems scan for companies that recently raised money. That signal is noisy — a company that closed a financing 90 days ago might or might not be ready to spend on awareness. The sharper signal is whether the company is already spending on awareness today. Four observable triggers:

SIGNAL 1 — IR firm hire announcements

A company that just engaged investor relations is in awareness-buying mode. PR is the logical next vendor.

SIGNAL 2 — Marketing / digital agency hire

Same pattern. Money is flowing toward visibility. PRmediaNow slots into the awareness stack.

SIGNAL 3 — Paid YouTube segment appearance

The company is already paying a digital agency for awareness. PR is a natural addition to their existing spend.

SIGNAL 4 — Broker-paid research initiation

Another commercial-spending signal. The company is investing in its capital-markets story.

After the briefing: outreach options

Once a candidate is approved, the outreach itself is PRmediaNow's call. Three patterns work depending on the relationship and TridentRise's role at the time:

PRmediaNow reaches out

The engine surfaces and prepares. PRmediaNow's principal sends the suggested angle as the principal-to-principal note.

TridentRise reaches out

Where appropriate, TridentRise sends the first note using a delegated address — useful when the principal's time is the constraint.

Hybrid

TridentRise sends the initial outreach; the principal joins the conversation once interest is confirmed. Common pattern for warm-path candidates.
