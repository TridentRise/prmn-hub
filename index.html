METHODOLOGY · SIGNAL MODEL
How your engine sources leads

Every cycle scans for companies that are already spending on awareness — the sharpest signal that they are ready for PR work. Most conventional lead-detection systems scan for who recently raised money; that signal is noisy. A company that closed a financing 90 days ago might or might not be spending on awareness. A company that just hired an IR firm, engaged a marketing agency, appeared on a paid YouTube segment, or bought broker-paid research is definitively in awareness-buying mode. Four observable triggers:

SIGNAL 1 — IR firm hire announcements

A company that just engaged investor relations is in awareness-buying mode. PR is the logical next vendor.

SIGNAL 2 — Marketing / digital agency hire

Same pattern. Money is flowing toward visibility. PRmediaNow slots into the awareness stack.

SIGNAL 3 — Paid YouTube segment appearance

The company is already paying a digital agency for awareness. PR is a natural addition to their existing spend.

SIGNAL 4 — Broker-paid research initiation

Another commercial-spending signal. The company is investing in its capital-markets story.

Every cycle, your engine scans the following sources for these signals:
